Echoes of the Hihi haha city

Characters 

Aerlin. An elderly elf who looks like a child, but her personality reveals a mature woman. Once she was in her adult form, who loved a man, but after she revealed him her feelings he rejected her heart. After that, with a broken heart, Aerlin decided to be a child forever and changed her appearance.

Tutu Josh. A human god, who tricked the Death and now he is still alive after thousands of years in Underworld. He travels with Aerlin, secretaly in love with her: when a man who was an object of Aerlin's love rejected her feelings, Tutu Josh killed him, but Aerlin do not know about this. 

Locations

The Old Older Forest. Dark forest that was a garden of gods millions years ago, after that it was burned during The Great War, and after thousands os years being deserted it started to recover. Now it is a healthy dark forest, very shadowy, with old trees. This forest hides the Underworld's Gate.

Hihi haha city. A human city that was founded not long ago, but developed into a huge center of commerce. This is a place where Aerlin meets Tutu Josh after thousands of years that Tutu Josh spended in the Underworld trying to trick the Death.

In Liyue Harbor during the annual Rite of Descension the Geo Archon Rex Lapis suddenly falls from the sky, apparently dead. Branded as suspects by the Millelith, the Traveler and Paimon are rescued by the Fatui Harbinger Childe, who advises them to seek the aid of the adepti in Jueyun Karst and Wangshu Inn to clear their names. There, they gain the attention of several adepti and ally with the erudite consultant Zhongli. Together, they decide to organize a traditional Rite of Parting to properly honor the fallen god, embarking on a journey across Liyue to gather sacred ritual items.

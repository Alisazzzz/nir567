Caroline Eilander and her husband, Nicholas, live on Paradise Island with Nicholas’ mother, Margaret, his brother, Gerard, and the couple’s three children: Elizabeth, David, and Jakob. 
Caroline is an alchemist and develops the recipe for the elixir, using the Lake as a basis. However, her family wishes to maintain Paradise Island through a ritual 
that requires a human sacrifice. They wish to sacrifice Jakob, but Caroline, loving her first-born son, sends him away on a boat. 
The family sacrifices Caroline instead.
